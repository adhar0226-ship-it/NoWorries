Why we exist

To replace guesswork with real answers, so food is planned for the people who are actually coming.

The problem

Nobody knows the real number.

Invitations go out, replies trickle in across calls and chats, and the kitchen still has to commit. That uncertainty costs everyone.

  • Food goes to waste

    Cooking extra "just in case" leaves good food, effort and money behind.

  • Or it runs short

    An unexpected rush can leave guests waiting and families embarrassed.

  • Hosts carry the stress

    The people who should be celebrating end up chasing replies and headcounts.

Who we're for

Three people. One shared picture.

When everyone sees the same information, nobody has to guess.

Hosts

Create your event, see RSVPs in one place and stay with your family instead of your phone.

Guests

Reply in moments and share your food preferences, so there is something on the table for you.

Caterers

Plan quantities around real counts and let the host know when each food category is ready.

How we get there

From invitation to last plate.

  1. Invite

    The host sets up the event and shares access with guests.

  2. RSVP

    Guests confirm whether they are coming and what they eat.

  3. Prepare

    The caterer plans the kitchen around those numbers.

  4. Celebrate

    Food readiness is visible, and the family can simply enjoy.

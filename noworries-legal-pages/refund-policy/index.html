2. Cancellation

Send a written request to [SUPPORT EMAIL]. A full refund applies if you cancel within [NUMBER] days of payment and the paid service has not started. After that, we may deduct the value of work already delivered and any non-recoverable costs disclosed to you before payment.

3. Eligible refunds

  • Duplicate payment or an amount charged in error.
  • A paid service we cancel or fail to provide.
  • A material fault on our side that we cannot fix within a reasonable time (full or proportionate refund).

4. Non-refundable

  • Services already fully delivered as agreed.
  • Changes in guest numbers, menu choices or event plans that do not affect our paid service.
  • Problems caused by incorrect information or misuse by the customer.

These exclusions do not limit remedies you have under consumer law, including for deficient service.

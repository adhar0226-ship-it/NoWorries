Cookie Policy

Cookies and similar browser storage used on NOWORRIES.

Last updated: 27 November 2026

On this page 1. The basics2. What we use3. Third parties4. Your controls5. Changes and contact

1. The basics

Cookies are small files a website can save in your browser. Websites can also use other storage, such as IndexedDB, local storage and session storage.

2. What we use

NOWORRIES does not use advertising or analytics cookies. We use storage that is necessary for the portals to work:

  • Sign-in session: Firebase Authentication keeps you signed in using your browser's persistent storage until you sign out or clear it.
  • Selected event (Host Portal): session storage remembers your active event until you sign out or close the tab.

3. Third parties

Google (Firebase and Google Fonts), GitHub (website hosting) and Render (backend hosting) receive technical data such as your IP address when your browser connects to their services. Their own policies govern that processing and any storage they set.

4. Your controls

You can view, block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings. Blocking necessary storage or clearing it may sign you out or stop parts of the portals working. If we add non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required before using them.

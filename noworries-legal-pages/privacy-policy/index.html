1. Who we are

NOWORRIES is a wedding RSVP and food-planning platform for Hosts, Guests and Caterers, operated by [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. This policy covers our website and portals. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and, as its provisions take effect, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

  • Hosts: name, email, phone number and password.
  • Guests: name of the head of family, address, email, phone number and password.
  • Caterers: business name, contact person, email, phone number and password.
  • Event details: couple names and photos, venue, date, timeline, dress code, messages, accommodation information, menu settings and preparation status.
  • RSVP details: number attending, dietary categories (such as vegetarian, vegan, Jain or Satvik), meal choices, allergies and intolerances, free-text notes and accommodation needs.
  • Technical data: IP address (used for rate limiting and abuse prevention) and server logs of requests and errors.

Allergy and dietary details may reveal health information or religious practice. Share only what the kitchen needs, and do not put names or other identifying details in free-text notes.

3. How we use it

We use data to create and secure accounts, manage invitations and attendance, calculate food and accommodation counts, show kitchen preparation status, send password reset emails, answer requests and meet legal duties. By creating an account and submitting information you consent to this use. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

4. Who can see it

  • Hosts see the guest family name, response status, attendance, dietary counts and accommodation needs for their event, plus combined allergy counts and notes.
  • Caterers see event details and combined food requirements, including free-text allergy notes. They are not shown guest names, contact details or accommodation records.
  • Guests see the host's name and the event details the host publishes, not other guests' responses.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect users and the platform.

5. Service providers

We rely on Google Firebase (authentication, database and photo storage), Render (backend hosting), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google Fonts (typefaces). They may process data, including your IP address, on servers outside India under their own terms and privacy policies.

6. Retention

We keep account, event and RSVP data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Data is not currently deleted automatically after an event. You can ask us to delete it using the contact below.

7. Security

Measures include encrypted connections (HTTPS), authentication provided by Firebase, role-based access checks, hashed event PINs and rate limits. No system is completely secure. Keep your password, Event ID and PIN private, and tell us promptly about any suspected misuse. We will notify affected people and authorities of data breaches as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct, update or erase your data, withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights and raise a grievance. Withdrawing consent or erasing data may stop parts of the service working. We may need to verify your identity. If we do not resolve your grievance, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once that remedy applies.

9. Children

Accounts are for people aged 18 or over. A parent or guardian may include children in a family's headcount and food needs. We do not knowingly let children create accounts.
